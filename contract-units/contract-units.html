What is Contract Units?

Contract Units is a solo project devoloped by oralei, currently a private/closed play-testing demo built in Unity. Take place in an free for all arena up to 8 players, selecting a Unit and weapon loadout to eliminate your opponents. The game is heavily inspired by titles like Titanfall 2 and Call of Duty: Black Ops III applying many elements from both games into Contract Units. The core game is meant to bridge the gap between competitive shooters, and having casual fun with friends.

What is the game about?

The Contract Units are a global private militia. If you have the funds, you can hire a "Unit" to get your dirty work done, whether that's infiltrating a government property or guarding a political figure. They come from all over the world, each specializing in a different area, but they all share one skill: eliminating their opponents, who are often other Contract Units.
