What is Contract Units?

Contract Units is a solo project devoloped by oralei, currently in a private/closed play-testing state. Take part in an free for all arena up to 8 players, selecting a Unit and weapon loadout to eliminate your opponents. The game is heavily inspired by titles like Titanfall 2 and Call of Duty: Black Ops III applying many elements from both games into Contract Units. The core game is meant to bridge the gap between competitive shooters, and having casual fun with friends.

Progress

This game begun development in May 2026, starting off initially as a BO3 movement recreation with multiplayer support. In June 2026, I initially wanted to fully nail down the movement mechanics and create racing gamemode. Shortly after the initial steps in June as, I decided I wanted to go full throttle with the FPS genre, implementing the weapon and health system. All the way from then and now, the core gameplay of Contract Units is a movement-based shooter. As months went on, more features were implemented and soon enough the project had progressed into a "playable" state.

About the game

Gameplay

Exo Movement

Wall run, slide, and jetpack your way across the map. Easy to learn, difficult to master.

Gunplay

Snappy, satisfying weapons with distinct calibres, damage falloff and predefined recoil patterns.

Units

Pick a Contract Unit, each with their own gadget, ultimate and playstyle.

Story

The Contract Units are a global private militia. If you have the funds, you can hire a "Unit" to get your dirty work done, whether that's infiltrating a government property or guarding a political figure. They come from all over the world, each specializing in a different area, but they all share one skill: eliminating their opponents, who are often other Contract Units.
