Gameplay

Contract Units is a game like no other. It's fully dynamic: you decide whether to pick gunfights that are slow and methodical or high energy and scrappy.

  • Gunplay

    Snappy and satisfying.

  • Movement

    Mechanical yet fluid. Easy to learn, difficult to master.

  • Hero-shooter meets rogue-lite

    Both intertwined with the core gameplay.

Please note: All gameplay shown below is subject to change. Artstyle, animations, sound effects, and visuals are all a work in progress and the game contains many placeholder assets.

Gunplay

Like any FPS, your aim is your most important asset. Contract Units places gunplay above all else, with satisfying and precise shooting mechanics.

Basic weapon mechanics

  • No accuracy penalty in the air

    Moving in the air doesn't cost you accuracy, so you can fight in the skies if you so wish.

  • No tagging or hit-slow

    Traverse in and out of battle fluidly, without stopping dead in your tracks from a little bit of damage.

Movement

Movement is an essential skill to learn. Mastering it lets you outmaneuver your opponents in creative and unexpected ways.

Basic movement in live gameplay

Units

There will be a wide range of unique characters to choose from. Each Unit has a kit, equipped with a Gadget and an Ultimate ability.

  • Gadget

    A renewable ability that refreshes based on a cooldown.

  • Ultimate

    Charged slowly over time, and mainly obtained through damaging enemy players.

Future goalsCore Gameplay Loop

The main gamemode will be a round-based free for all with up to 8 players.

  1. One life per round

    Dying eliminates you for the remainder of the round.

  2. Earn points

    Eliminating enemy players rewards you points towards a meter.

  3. Outlast

    Being the last player remaining also rewards points.

  4. Close it out

    At maximum points, you must win the round in order to win the match.

Other gamemode variants: there will likely be others, such as kill-point based rounds, where respawning is allowed and the winner of the round is whoever hits the kill goal first.
