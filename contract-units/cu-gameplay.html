Gameplay

Contract Units is a game like no other. It's fully dynamic, where you decide to pick gunfights that are slow and methodical or high energy and scrappy. The core gameplay of Contract Units focuses on snappy and satisfying gunplay, mixed with mechanical yet fluid movement that is easy to learn, difficult to master.

Please note: All gameplay shown below is subject to change. Artstyle, animations, sound effects, and visuals are all a work in progress and the game contains many placeholder assets.

Gunplay

Like any FPS, your aim is the most important asset in winning against your opponents. Contract Units is no different, placing gunplay first above all else with satisfying and precise shooting mechanics. In Contract Units, there is no accuracy penalty for moving in air, allowing you to fight in the skies if you so wish. The game also does not include "tagging" or "hit-slow", allowing you to traverse in and out of battle fluidly without stopping dead in your tracks just from a little bit of damage

Units

In Contract Units, there will be a wide range of unique characters to choose from. Each Unit has a kit, equipped with a Gadget and Ultimate ability. Gadget's are renewable abilities that refresh based on a cooldown, while Ultimates are charged slowly overtime and mainly obtained through damaging enemy players.

Future goals

Core Gameplay Loop

The main gamemode will be a round-based free for all up to 8 players where dying eliminates you for the remainder of the round. Eliminating enemy players or being the last player remaining rewards you points towards a meter. At maximum points, you must win the round in order to win the match.

There will likley be other gamemodes, such as kill-point based rounds, where respawning is allowed, and the winner of the round is whomever hits the kill goal first.

Mayhem
